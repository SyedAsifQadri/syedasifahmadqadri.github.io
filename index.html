I'm an AI researcher based in Hsinchu, Taiwan, working at the intersection of computer vision and trustworthy deep learning. My work focuses on a deceptively hard problem: models that perform brilliantly in the lab often fail when deployed in the messy, shifting conditions of the real world.

To close that gap, I design domain-adaptive architectures that generalize across imaging environments, uncertainty-aware frameworks that know when not to trust their own predictions, and generative pipelines that synthesize the data reality doesn't provide. I also care deeply about deployment, compressing and quantizing models to run on embedded and edge devices with real-time constraints.

My research journey began with speech and audio AI, building deep learning systems for speech emotion recognition and depression prediction from voice, a thread I still carry through ongoing collaborations on audio deepfake detection. That multimodal perspective shapes how I think about perception today.

My work has been published in venues including IEEE Transactions journals, Elsevier, ACM workshops and Springer, spanning visual inspection, anomaly detection, multimodal fusion, and speech analysis.

featured frameworks

Three answers to one question

How do you make a vision model survive the real world? Each framework attacks the gap from a different angle.

DDAN-RGAL

problem → field images look nothing like training data

A Dual-Domain Adaptive Network with Reality-Gap Aware Learning. It explicitly models the lab-to-field shift, aligning features across both domains so detection holds up in deployment conditions it has never seen.

DPB

problem → models are confidently wrong on hard cases

Difficulty-Aware Domain Bridging with Monotonic Uncertainty. It ranks samples by difficulty and guarantees uncertainty grows as inputs get harder, so a prediction's confidence score finally means something you can act on.

ESDNet & the Epidemiological Pipeline

problem → one label can't describe a complex symptom

An evidential network that decomposes what it sees into symptom evidences with Dirichlet-based uncertainty, paired with an epidemiologically-constrained generative pipeline: constrained GAN synthesis, disease-aware super-resolution, knowledge fusion and dynamic multi-task learning.
